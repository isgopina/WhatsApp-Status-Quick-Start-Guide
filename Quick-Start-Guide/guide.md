## Overview

WhatsApp Status shares temporary updates with contacts through the Updates screen. This guide explains various status formats and the actions required to publish each update.

## About WhatsApp Status

WhatsApp Status provides a temporary space for sharing messages and media with selected contacts. Contacts view available updates through the Updates screen without receiving individual chat messages.

**Status Formats**

Status supports photo, Layout, Music, Voice, Video, Text, and AI image formats. Each format provides a different way to present information.

**How Status Works**

Each published update remains visible for 24 hours before WhatsApp removes the update. Contacts can respond privately to an update.

**Status audience**

Status privacy settings determine which contacts can view an update. The Status (Contacts) control displays audience options before publication. Changes to audience settings apply to later updates, not updates that already appear.

## Share WhatsApp Status updates

**Before You Begin:**

- Open WhatsApp on an Android or iPhone device.
- You need a stable internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data)
- Allow access to photos, camera, and microphone when the format requires access.
- You also need the latest version of WhatsApp
- Confirm that the layout, music, or AI images appear on your account before using those topics.

> **Note:** The interface varies across devices and releases. Confirm the labels on your phone before you submit.

### Updating Your Status with a Photo

Share a saved picture. Add text, a caption, or stickers before you post.

**Prerequisites:**

- A saved photo or camera access
- Photo library access for WhatsApp

To share a photo status, perform the following steps:

1. On the WhatsApp home screen, tap the **Updates** tab.
2. Next to **My Status**, tap the **Add Status** icon.
3. Select a photo from your gallery or tap the **Camera** icon to take a new photo.
4. (Optional:) Use the editing tools to add text, stickers, or drawings to the photo. You can also crop the photo or add music to the photo.
5. Tap the **Send** icon.

> **Note:** Media quality can decrease when WhatsApp processes a status.

**Result:** Your photo appears in My Status and is visible for 24 hours.

### Create a status collage using Layout

Combine two to six photos in one update. Select a layout before sharing.

**Prerequisites:**

- Two to six saved photos
- Access to the Layout option

To share a status collage, perform the following steps:

1. On **Updates**, tap **My status.**
2. In the status creation menu, tap **Layout.**
3. In the photo picker, perform the following steps:
   - Select two to six photos.
   - Tap **Check.**
4. In the collage editor, tap a layout option.

   You can also perform the following:
   - Select another layout to compare the arrangement.
   - Tap **Status (Contacts)** to review the audience.
5. In the collage editor, tap **Send.**

> **Note:** Layout places multiple photos in a single update. The available arrangements depend on the selected photos.

**Result:** The collage appears as one status update for 24 hours.

### Share a song using Music

Share a song as a Status update without selecting a picture. Choose the track from WhatsApp’s music library.

**Prerequisites:**

- An active internet connection
- Access to the Music option and licensed song library

To share a music status, perform the following steps:

1. On **Updates,** tap **Add status.**
2. In the status creation menu, tap Music.
3. In the music browser, tap the search field
4. In the search field, enter a song title or artist.
5. (Optional:) In the results, tap **Play** to preview the track.
6. In the results, select song.
7. In the clip editor, drag the timeline to select a music segment.
8. (Optional:) In the status editor, tap **Status (Contacts)** to review the audience.
9. In the status editor, tap **Send**.

> **Note:** Music availability and song selection vary by country and account type.

> **Note:** The music tool also supports photo and video statuses.

**Result:** The selected song appears in your status for 24 hours.

### Record a status using Voice

Record a spoken message without creating a video. Change the background color before posting.

**Prerequisites:**

- Microphone access for WhatsApp
- A quiet place to record the message

To share a voice status, perform the following steps:

1. On **Updates**, tap **Text edit**.
2. In the text editor, press and hold **Microphone** to record.
3. Release **Microphone** to stop recording.
4. (Optional:) In the voice editor, tap **Color** to change the background.
5. (Optional:) In the voice editor, tap **Status (Contacts)** to review the audience.
6. In the voice editor, tap **Send**.

> **Note:** Voice statuses do not support private mentions in the current WhatsApp Help Center instructions.

**Result:** The voice recording appears in your status for 24 hours.

### Update status using a video

Share a saved video or capture a new clip. Trim the selected video before sharing.

**Prerequisites:**

- A saved video or camera access
- Video library access for WhatsApp

To share a video status, perform the following steps:

1. On **Updates**, tap **Add status**.
2. In the video picker, select a video.

   > **Note:** The Camera and Video options allow new recordings. The Help Center lists a 90-second limit.
3. In the video editor, drag the trim slider to select the clip.
4. (Optional:) In the video editor, tap **Text**.
5. (Optional:) In the text editor, type text over the video.
6. (Optional:) In the video editor, tap **Stickers** to add a sticker.
7. (Optional:) In the video editor, tap **Add a caption…**.
8. (Optional:) In the caption field, enter a caption.
9. (Optional:) In the video editor, tap **Status (Contacts)** to review the audience.
10. In the video editor, tap **Send**.

**Result:** The video appears in your status for 24 hours.

### Update status using text

Share a written message without selecting a photo. Change the font or background color before posting.

To share a text status, perform the following steps:

1. On **Updates**, tap **Text edit**.
2. In the text editor, enter the message.
3. (Optional:) In the text editor, tap **Text** to change the font.
4. (Optional:) In the text editor, tap **Color** to change the background.
5. (Optional:) In the text editor, tap **Stickers** to insert a sticker.
6. (Optional:) In the text editor, tap **Status (Contacts)** to review the audience.
7. In the text editor, tap **Send**.

> **Note:** WhatsApp currently excludes private mentions from text statuses.

**Result:** The text message appears in your status for 24 hours.

## Create a status using AI images

Generate a picture from a text prompt or choose a suggested picture. This option depends on Meta AI availability.

**Prerequisites:**

- Access to Meta AI and the AI images option
- An internet connection
- A short description for a custom image

To share an AI-generated status image, perform the following steps:

1. On **Updates**, tap **Add status**.
2. In the status creation menu, tap **AI images**.
3. In the image generator, enter a text prompt describing the picture.
4. In the generated results, select an image.
5. (Optional:) In the status editor, tap **Add a caption…**.
6. (Optional:) In the caption field, enter a caption.
7. (Optional:) In the status editor, tap **Status (Contacts)** to review the audience.
8. In the status editor, tap **Send**.

> **Note:** Suggested images provide an alternative to entering a prompt.

> **Note:** The AI images option may be unavailable on some accounts or in some regions.

> **Note:** Review generated content for errors before sharing.

**Result:** The selected generated image appears in your status for 24 hours.

## FAQs

**Q. How long does photo status stay visible?**

A. The photo status is visible to your contacts for 24 hours and is removed automatically.

**Q. Can I edit a photo after I post my status?**

A. No. To change your photo status, delete it and post a new one.

**Q. Who can see my Status?**

A. By default, your status is visible to your contacts. You can change this status privacy in settings.

## Troubleshoot status updates

Check the affected feature when a status fails to post or an option is missing.

**A status does not post**

Confirm the internet connection. Reopen the status editor and retry the update.

**A photo or video does not appear in the picker**

Check WhatsApp permissions for photos and videos in the phone settings.

**The microphone does not record**

Check microphone access in the phone settings. Retry Voice after allowing access.

**Music, Layout, or AI images is missing**

Check the available features in the installed WhatsApp version. Feature availability varies across accounts and regions.

**The wrong audience can view a new update**

Review Status (Contacts) before posting. An earlier update retains its original audience settings.

## Best Practices

- Use good lighting so your photo is clear before posting.
- Check your Status privacy setting before posting anything.
- Keep text captions short so they remain readable and don’t cover your photo.
